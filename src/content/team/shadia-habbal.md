Professor of solar physics at the Institute for Astronomy in Honolulu. Habbal founded the Solar Wind Sherpas in 1995 and has led more than twenty scientific eclipse expeditions. Her research uses imaging and spectroscopy during totality to diagnose the corona, the solar wind, and coronal mass ejections.
